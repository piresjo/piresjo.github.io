About Me

When it comes to software, user experience is paramount. Having worked on customer-facing projects my entire career, user experience has been at the forefront of my work.

For the past few years, I have worked at FanDuel, driving projects and designing features that have either improved the user’s experience or expanded our market (or, oftentimes, both). At FanDuel, I worked on developing two new products: Cartola FC and Faceoff. Cartola FC brought FanDuel’s Daily Fantasy Sports (DFS) ecosystem to Brazil, bringing in many customers betting on Brazil’s Serie A league and the World Cup. With Faceoff, the combination of skill-based games, the DFS ecosystem, and FanDuel’s account ecosystem brought in many users who would not have engaged with a FanDuel product, leading to hundreds of thousands of unique users using Faceoff.

Prior to FanDuel, I was working at Amazon, developing new features for Alexa’s reminder functionality. Before that, I was developing new features for the alarm functionality.

Throughout my career, I have worked with a variety of technologies under a series of different focuses. Customer experience has been paramount, but it was not the only thing I focused on. Scalability, performance, and sustainability have also been key points in my work. This was pivotal for large-traffic events, including the Super Bowl, the World Cup, or Prime Day.

If you would like to reach out, feel free to message me or send me an email; I am always available to chat.
